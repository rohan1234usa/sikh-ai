What the site is, in its own words

The values are not aspirational. They are enforced in code: in the system prompt, the citation checker, the privacy switch, the WCAG notes in the stylesheet. A fitting UI makes the same commitments visible without labels.

One structural fact runs through every surface: the three-line unit of Gurmukhi, romanization and English. The Hukamnama, each Ang, the citation cards, the translator's result and every vocabulary word are all that unit. Whatever scheme wins, it should be designed as one component, once.

As it is today

The current tokens are the Nishan Sahib's colours, which is right. The idiom they are used in is the 2023 AI start-up: a navy gradient hero with blurred glow orbs, a floating mock chat window with macOS traffic lights, a "Powered by Google Gemini" badge, rounded cards with shadows, and bouncing dots.

What it says against the values

  • The first screen shows the vendor and a fake conversation. It shows no Gurbani at all.
  • Gurmukhi is a fallback font bolted onto a developer typeface, then set bold and centred on every page. It shouts rather than reads.
  • Quoted Gurbani inside a reply looks exactly like the AI's own words. The check box has to say what the type should already show.
  • Every page opens with the same navy band and centred title; the surfaces have no distinct register.
  • Dark mode is Tailwind's slate-950, which every other app also has.

What is already right

  • Kesri and navy belong to the site and to the logo, favicon and share image. A scheme can keep them.
  • The accessibility rigour: contrast-checked tokens, focus rings, scroll padding, reduced motion.
  • The three-line tukk is already consistent in content, if not yet in form.
Scheme 1 · chosen

Pothiਪੋਥੀ

The Word is the interface. Every screen is treated as a page of a bir. Gurbani is set in the manuscript register: a text face made for Gurmukhi, a generous measure, rubrics in vermilion for the raag and the Ang, gold hairlines instead of boxes. Everything else, the AI's replies, the tools and the chrome, is marginalia: smaller, quieter, in the same family. The hierarchy of trust the system prompt enforces becomes visible without a single label.

Type

ਗੁਰੁ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਰਤਨੀ ਭਰਪੂਰੇ ॥

Wisdom of the Gurus

Tiro Gurmukhi carries both scripts in one family, so a quoted tukk inside an English sentence no longer changes voice.

Gurbani, Punjabi and running Latin: Tiro Gurmukhi. Display: Newsreader.

System

  • No cards, no shadows. Hairline rules, margins and a folio header do the structuring.
  • Square corners. One ornament only, a border motif from illuminated birs, at the top of a page.
  • Chat is a transcript, not bubbles. Your line is marked in vermilion; quoted Gurbani is indented on a gold rule; the Gurbani check becomes numbered footnotes.
  • Motion: a fade. Nothing floats, bounces or glows.

On each surface

Reading
Date, raag and Ang as a folio header; the tukk block; translation beneath in the display face.
Chat
Source quotes look like the Hukamnama page; the AI's prose looks like a commentary.
Tools
Same family at small sizes; results as ruled rows, three scripts in three columns.
Learn
Letter tiles as ruled boxes; progress as a gold rule that fills.
Seva
Notices on a board, double-hairline frames, the category stamped in vermilion.

Why it fits

  • It is the only scheme whose structure, not its labels, says "source above machine".
  • Most of the site is reading. A text face made for Gurmukhi, at a real measure, is the biggest comfort gain available.
  • Calm enough for the hardship and grief prompts; night mode suits Amrit Vela reading.
  • One family for three scripts solves the fallback-font problem at the root.
  • Ink and eggshell clear AA with room to spare, by day and by night.

Risks

  • Drifts toward the cream-and-serif look every design tool now produces, and eggshell sits close to that cream. Lapis and vermilion rather than terracotta, the gold band, square geometry and modern density are what keep it a manuscript rather than a template.
  • Forms and the translator need discipline so they feel like the apparatus of a scholarly edition, not an antique.
Scheme 2

Nishanਨਿਸ਼ਾਨ

The flag, at poster scale. Keep the site's own colours, the Nishan Sahib's kesri and blue, but use them the way a flag does: flat fields, hard edges, no gradient, no glow, no shadow. Type carries the ornament, set in a variable superfamily designed for Gurmukhi and Latin together. The home page's feature grid becomes a pangat: every cell the same size, nothing above anything else.

Type

ਗੁਰੁ ਸਾਗਰੁ ਰਤਨੀ ਭਰਪੂਰੇ ॥

Wisdom of the Gurus

Anek was drawn as one family across nine Indian scripts and Latin, with a width axis, so labels can go narrow and headlines wide without changing face.

Everything: Anek Gurmukhi and Anek Latin (Ek Type), weight 100 to 800, width 75 to 125.

System

  • Zero radius. Four-pixel kesri rules. One-pixel blue gridlines.
  • Gurmukhi letterforms at architectural size, cropped at the edge, as the only graphic.
  • Chat is a transcript with a kesri rule for your turns and a blue rule for SikhAI's; quoted Gurbani in the heaviest cut.
  • Motion: an underline that sweeps in kesri. Nothing else.

On each surface

Reading
The tukk huge on a kesri field; translation in blue on white beneath.
Chat
White and blue only; kesri marks you, never the AI.
Tools
Dense ruled tables, numerals tabular, labels narrow and uppercase.
Learn
Flat blue squares that turn kesri when learned.
Seva
Grid cells, category as a solid tag, dates in the heavy cut.

Why it fits

  • Identity-proud and in chardi kala; the colours are already the brand, so the logo, icons and share image survive untouched.
  • Flat, high-contrast fields are the easiest system to keep at AA in both modes.
  • Strong at phone width, where most Sangat will read it.
  • It removes the start-up gloss without borrowing anyone else's heritage.

Risks

  • Loud for grief and hardship conversations. Kesri must be a signal on one element per view, never wallpaper; conversation and reading stay white and blue.
  • Kesri at field scale can read as political rather than devotional. The giant Gurmukhi, not a khanda, is what keeps it about the Word.

Wisdom of the Gurus

A serif Gurmukhi gives the tukk an inscribed, carved quality; the humanist body face stays open and quiet around it.

Gurbani: Noto Serif Gurmukhi. Body in both scripts: Mukta Mahee. Display: Cormorant Garamond.

System

  • Open space; large radius on the few containers that need one; thin gold rules.
  • A pietra dura inlay motif as a one-pixel ornament, never as a background.
  • Soft bubbles with a lot of air; quoted Gurbani between two gold hairlines.
  • Motion: a slow light sweep across the Hukamnama header, off under reduced motion.

On each surface

Reading
Centred serif Gurmukhi, translation beneath, a short gold rule between lines.
Chat
Teal for you, white for SikhAI, gold for the Guru's words.
Tools
White panels on marble, controls in water teal.
Learn
Tiles like inlay stones; progress as a gold line.
Seva
White cards, a gold eyebrow for the category.

Why it fits

  • "A space designed for peace" is the site's own promise; this is the scheme that keeps it most literally.
  • The Hukamnama page becomes a place, the one the Hukam actually comes from.
  • Gentlest register for the hardship and grief prompts.
  • Carries diaspora memory of Darbar Sahib without a single photograph.

Risks

  • Slides easily into the wellness-app look. The serif Gurmukhi and the inlay geometry are what hold it to Sikhi.
  • Gold fails AA at text sizes unless deepened, which is why there are two golds.
  • Too soft for the translator and the Seva forms unless those borrow a plainer register.
Scheme 4

Phulkariਫੁਲਕਾਰੀ

Thread, geometry and the grandmother's craft. For people who grew up hearing Punjabi at home, the interface draws on the home's textile rather than a classroom. The darning stitch of phulkari gives a geometry, lozenges, bands and stitched edges, and a palette, mustard floss on madder and indigo. Pattern lives in bands and edges; text always sits on plain ground.

Baloo Paaji is named for the Punjabi honorific and drawn for both scripts; it is warm at display size and would be too playful for running text, which stays in Mukta Mahee.

Why it fits

  • Warmth, family and community: Learn and Seva are the surfaces that shine here.
  • Joy and chardi kala in the palette itself.
  • Unmistakably Punjabi and unlike any AI product on the market.

Risks

  • Pattern fights reading; the folk register can feel too casual for scripture. Gurbani pages must stay plain.
  • Five hues mean five times the contrast work across two modes.
  • Cut-corner bubbles clip borders; the construction needs care.

Recommendation

Pothi as the backbone, in the Nishan Sahib's colours.

Take Pothi's structure and type whole: Tiro Gurmukhi with Newsreader, an eggshell ground with ink, rules instead of cards, a transcript instead of bubbles, the Gurbani check as footnotes. Then swap its vermilion for kesri and keep a deep blue as the interactive colour. At night it turns inside out: eggshell text on an ink ground, with gold rules and light lapis links. Kesri then needs two values. By day, deepen the site's kesri-deep a touch to #AD4E08, because the current value only just reaches 4.5:1 on eggshell. At night, use the full kesri, which reaches 8.5:1 on ink, where kesri-deep would fail. Kesri stays the brand's colour, so the logo, favicon and share image still belong, and the brand reads as the same site grown up.

  1. It is the only scheme that encodes the deepest value structurally. Source above machine is a typographic hierarchy here, not a caption on a box.
  2. Most of the product is reading. The Hukamnama, every Ang, every citation and every translation result benefit from a face made for Gurmukhi at a real measure.
  3. It stays calm where the site must be calm: the hardship and grief prompts, and night reading at Amrit Vela.
  4. One family for three scripts ends the fallback-font arrangement that currently makes Gurmukhi look like a guest.
  5. It is the furthest from the AI start-up look while remaining the easiest to keep at AA.

Borrow two moments from the others: Nishan's equal-cell pangat grid for the home page's features, and Sarovar's single dawn gradient for the Hukamnama header only. Nothing else from them.

Moves that hold whichever scheme wins

  • The hero becomes the day's Hukamnama, live. Retire the mock chat window, the traffic lights and the Gemini badge; Gemini belongs in the footer and the privacy page.
  • Build the tukk once, as one component with a fixed rhythm, and use it on the Hukamnama, Angs, citations, translator results and vocabulary words.
  • Three voices with three registers: Source, SikhAI, you. Gurbani quoted inside a reply must look like Source.
  • Choose the Gurmukhi face first and the Latin to match it. Never the reverse.
  • Retire the navy band every page currently opens with; let each surface open with its content.

How to get there without a rewrite

  • Tokens first: the semantic variables in globals.css already carry about 850 class uses across the site, so most of the palette changes in one file. About 150 literal navy, kesri and slate classes in 45 files wait for the chrome step.
  • Fonts second: fonts.ts loads three families; swapping them and the sans and Gurmukhi tokens re-sets every page. Tiro Gurmukhi has a single weight, so the five places that set Gurmukhi in bold drop it rather than get a synthesised bold.
  • The tukk component third, replacing the per-page markup on the Hukamnama, Ang and citation views.
  • Chrome fourth: navbar, footer, buttons.ts and the seva styles file.
  • Chat last: the transcript layout is the one structural change; everything before it is skin.

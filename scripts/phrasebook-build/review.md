- ਅੱਛਾ achha: The 'chh' (ਛ) is an aspirated 'ch' sound, pronounced with a strong puff of air, similar to the 'ch-h' in 'match hook'.

### `thorhi-punjabi` · everyday

Notes:
- grammar — Expressing language ability with 'auna': In Punjabi, knowing a language or skill is phrased as it 'coming' to you: 'Mainu [language] aundi hai' literally means 'Punjabi comes to me', rather than using an active verb like 'I know'.

Pronunciation:
- ਥੋੜ੍ਹੀ thorhi: The 'th' is an aspirated dental 't' (tip of tongue behind upper front teeth with a puff of air), and 'rh' (ੜ੍ਹ) is an aspirated retroflex flap made by curling the tongue tip back and flapping it down.

- ਮਤਲਬ matlab: The 't' here is a soft dental sound (tongue touching the upper front teeth), not the English alveolar 't'.
